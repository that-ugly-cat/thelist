These are counts, not hours. Open is what is on the list right now and done is what closed in the period, both weighted by declared size ({% for e, w in data.weights.items() %}{{ e }} = {{ w }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}) — which separates the chapter from the website update, and is still not time. The small grey figure is the plain row count behind each: while nobody touches the size menu it is exactly the weighted one divided by three, and you can see that here. Touches counts operations on a task — notes, edits, completions, reopenings — and never the act of creating it, so a day spent filling the board does not read as a day of work.

The three shares are not the same kind of number. Share of open and share of done divide weight, so a large task counts for eight small ones. Share of touches divides a plain count, and it counts a note exactly like a completion — which is the right reading of how often somebody handled these rows and the wrong reading of how much they did. It is also the one figure here that goes up by writing more, so read it as frequency and never as effort; the other two are untouched by it.
