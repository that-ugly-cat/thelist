TheList

A list of the things worth remembering exist — owned by one person, annotated by the people they work with.

One order, declared

The list is in the order the owner dragged it into. Deadlines are shown, never sorted on: an automatic reordering would make the dragging mean nothing.

Proposals, not edits

Editors add notes, reorder, mark things done and propose new items. The owner accepts or declines — and a declined proposal stays, with the reason.

Recurring without a scheduler

Some things just need doing every so often. Those stay on the list and record when they were last done, instead of spawning copies of themselves.

Who the work is for

Every row says on whose behalf it exists, so the quarter can be counted: how much was yours, how much was somebody else's.
